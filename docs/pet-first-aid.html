Pet First Aid

Pet First Aid Course

Course Fee: R750

Duration: 6 weeks

Purpose of the Course

This course aims to provide emergency first aid knowledge for pets. Learners will learn how to recognise common emergencies, give basic care safely and get a pet to a vet as quickly as possible.

What You'll Learn

  • Minor injuries
  • CPR basics
  • Poison awareness
  • Heatstroke
  • Emergency transport
