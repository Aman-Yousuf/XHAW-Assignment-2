About Pawsitive Pet Academy

History

Founded in Durban, Pawsitive Pet Academy started with a mission to bridge the gap between pet owners and professional animal care practices. Over the years, we have expanded from basic obedience training to comprehensive vocational courses covering grooming, health, and business skills.

Vision

To be South Africa's premier training academy for ethical pet care, fostering harmonious relationships between pets, owners, and professional service providers.

Mission

To empower pet owners and aspiring animal care professionals through accessible, high-quality, hands-on educational programs grounded in positive reinforcement and modern care standards.

Goals

  • Provide practical skill development for domestic pet care and management.
  • Promote humane animal handling and positive reinforcement training.
  • Support local entrepreneurs in establishing successful pet-related businesses across South Africa.
