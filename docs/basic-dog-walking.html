Basic Dog Walking Course

Course Fee: R750

Duration: 6 weeks

Purpose of the Course

This course aims to teach learners safe and professional dog walking practices. Learners will develop the skills needed to handle dogs responsibly, provide suitable exercise and ensure the safety of dogs and other people.

What You'll Learn

  • Walking equipment
  • Handling multiple dogs
  • Safety procedures
  • Exercise routines
