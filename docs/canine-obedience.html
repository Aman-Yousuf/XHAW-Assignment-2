Canine Obedience Training

Canine Obedience Training Course

Course Fee: R1,500

Duration: 6 months

Purpose of the Course

This course aims to teach learners effective dog obedience and behavioural training techniques. Learners will develop the skills needed to train dogs using positive methods and build a calm, well-behaved relationship between dogs and their owners.

What You'll Learn

  • Basic commands
  • Leash training
  • Behaviour correction
  • Positive reinforcement
  • Socialisation techniques
